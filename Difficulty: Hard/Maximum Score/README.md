<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/maximum-score--173045/1?page=12&difficulty=Hard&sortBy=accuracy">Maximum Score</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Hard</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p><span style="font-size: 14pt;">You are given an array <strong>arr[]</strong> of positive integers of size<strong> n</strong> and your initial score <strong>x</strong>. Now, for every subarray for size 1 to n, compute the sum of medians of all subarrays of that size. </span><span style="font-size: 14pt;">You are given one chance to improve your score.<br>In this, you must choose exactly <strong>k</strong> values from the computed median sums, perform a <strong>bitwise AND</strong> operation on them, and then perform a <strong>bitwise OR</strong> between the result and your initial score <strong>x</strong>.</span><br><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Return the maximum possible score you can achieve after performing this operation.</span></p>
<p><strong><span style="font-size: 14pt;">Examples:</span></strong></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input:&nbsp;</strong>n = 3, arr[] = [4, 3, 6], k = 2 , x = 7 <br></span><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Output:&nbsp;</strong>7</span><br><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Explanation: </strong>Sum of median of size = 1 is 13, size = 2 is 6, size = 3 is 4,  the <strong>AND</strong> of 13 and 6 is give 4, and since (4 | 7) = 7, the final answer is 7.we can not get more than that.</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Input: </strong>n = 3, arr[] = [1, 1, 2], k = 1 , x = 13<br></span><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Output: </strong>15</span><br><span style="font-size: 14pt;"><strong>Explanation: </strong>Sum of median of size = 1 is 4, size = 2 is 3, size = 3 is 1,and if we chose 3 then </span><span style="font-size: 14pt; font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Oxygen, Ubuntu, Cantarell, 'Open Sans', 'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif;"> </span><strong style="font-size: 14pt; font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Oxygen, Ubuntu, Cantarell, 'Open Sans', 'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif;">OR</strong><span style="font-size: 14pt; font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Oxygen, Ubuntu, Cantarell, 'Open Sans', 'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif;"> with </span><strong style="font-size: 14pt; font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Oxygen, Ubuntu, Cantarell, 'Open Sans', 'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif;">x</strong><span style="font-size: 14pt; font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Oxygen, Ubuntu, Cantarell, 'Open Sans', 'Helvetica Neue', sans-serif;">  is 15. We can not get more than 15.</span></pre>
